# Stakeholder Validation Questions

> **Historical validation guide (June 2026).** Use these questions for business discovery, but use the current [Demo Review Script](DEMO_REVIEW_SCRIPT.md) and [UAT Checklist](UAT_CHECKLIST.md) when evaluating the implemented MVP.

Version: 0.1
Date: 2026-06-25

Use this document to validate the MVP with school admin, finance staff, principal, and CEO before implementation begins.

The goal is not to ask every possible ERP question. The goal is to confirm the finance workflow that replaces Excel.

## 1. Current Excel Workflow

Ask the admin or finance staff:

1. How many Excel files are used for student fees?
2. Is there one file per year, per month, per class, or per school?
3. Who copies the worksheet each month?
4. What fields are manually changed every month?
5. How are receipt numbers currently assigned?
6. Has a duplicate receipt number happened before?
7. Which formulas are most likely to break?
8. How long does monthly invoice preparation usually take?
9. How is outstanding fee checked today?
10. What report is prepared for the principal or CEO every month?

Evidence to collect:

- A sample anonymized Excel file
- Current receipt sample
- Current invoice or fee statement sample
- Current monthly collection report
- Current outstanding report

## 2. Student and Parent Records

Ask:

1. What is the official student ID format?
2. Are student IDs unique only inside one school?
3. What student statuses are used today?
4. Do withdrawn students still appear in outstanding reports?
5. How are classes named?
6. Can one parent have multiple children in the school?
7. Does one student need multiple contacts, e.g. father, mother, guardian?
8. Which contact is used for billing?

MVP confirmation:

- Student has one current class.
- Student can have multiple parents or guardians.
- Only active students are included in invoice generation.

## 3. Fee Structure

Ask:

1. What are the standard fee items?
2. Which fees repeat monthly?
3. Which fees are one-time?
4. Can a student have a custom tuition amount?
5. Is transport charged to all students or selected students only?
6. Are meal/material/activity fees optional?
7. Does registration fee appear only once?
8. Are there fees that apply only to a specific month?

MVP confirmation:

- Fee items can be recurring or one-time.
- Student fee assignments can override default amounts.
- Invoice uses fee snapshot.

## 4. Discounts

Ask:

1. What discount types exist today?
2. Is sibling discount fixed amount or percentage?
3. Does sibling discount apply to tuition only or total invoice?
4. Can a student have more than one discount?
5. Do discounts have start and end dates?
6. Who can approve scholarship or custom discount?
7. Is discount reason required?

MVP confirmation:

- Discounts are assigned directly to students.
- Discounts support fixed amount and percentage.
- Default MVP applies discounts to the whole invoice.

## 5. Invoice Generation

Ask:

1. Are invoices generated monthly for all students?
2. Do some classes have different billing dates?
3. What is the normal due date?
4. Should admin preview invoices before generation?
5. What should happen if a student has no fee assigned?
6. Can invoice be changed after it is generated?
7. Can invoice be changed after payment is received?
8. Is invoice number required, or only receipt number matters?
9. What exact fields should appear on invoice PDF?

MVP confirmation:

- Generate invoices by school and month.
- Optional class filter is useful.
- Prevent duplicate invoice for same student and month.
- Invoice items are snapshots.

## 6. Payment Workflow

Ask:

1. What payment methods are currently accepted?
2. Do parents pay by cash, bank transfer, QR, or online?
3. Do parents often pay several invoices in one payment?
4. Do parents often pay for siblings in one payment?
5. Are partial payments common?
6. Is overpayment allowed?
7. How are bank transfer references recorded?
8. Does finance need to verify bank transfer before issuing receipt?
9. Is payment proof upload required?
10. What happens when payment was entered wrongly?

MVP confirmation:

- Payment supports cash, bank transfer, QR.
- Partial payment is supported.
- Database supports future multi-invoice allocation.
- Payment mistakes are voided, not deleted.

## 7. Receipt Workflow

Ask:

1. What is the official receipt number format?
2. Does receipt numbering reset every year?
3. Is receipt prefix different for each school?
4. Should receipt be generated automatically after payment?
5. Should bank transfer receipt wait for verification?
6. Can receipt be cancelled or voided?
7. Should voided receipt numbers be reused? Expected answer should be no.
8. What exact fields must appear on receipt PDF?
9. Who is allowed to void receipt?
10. Is receipt printed, downloaded, emailed, or sent by WhatsApp?

MVP confirmation:

- Receipt numbers are generated by backend.
- Receipt sequence is per school and year.
- Voided receipt numbers are not reused.

## 8. Reports and Dashboard

Ask:

1. What does finance check every morning?
2. What does principal want to see weekly or monthly?
3. What does CEO want across all schools?
4. What is the definition of monthly collection?
5. Should void payments be excluded from collection? Expected answer should be yes.
6. Should overdue mean after due date, or after month end?
7. What outstanding report columns are required?
8. Is student ledger required for parent disputes?
9. What reports must export to Excel?
10. What reports must export to PDF?

MVP confirmation:

- Dashboard prioritizes useful finance numbers.
- Reports are generated from invoice/payment/receipt records.

## 9. Permissions

Ask:

1. Who can create or edit students?
2. Who can assign fees?
3. Who can assign discounts?
4. Who can generate monthly invoices?
5. Who can record payment?
6. Who can void payment?
7. Who can void receipt?
8. Who can export reports?
9. Should CEO be view-only?
10. Should school admins see other schools?

MVP confirmation:

- School Admin manages own school.
- Finance manages payment and receipt workflow.
- CEO sees group-level reports.
- Super Admin manages all.

## 10. Deployment and Operations

Ask:

1. Who will use the system daily?
2. How many admin users are expected?
3. Is the system used only in school office or also remotely?
4. Is internet connection stable enough for cloud-based system?
5. Who should receive backup or export files?
6. How often should backup run?
7. Is there any data privacy requirement from the school?
8. Is bilingual UI required in phase 1?

## 11. Final Go / No-Go Questions

Before development starts, confirm:

1. Is replacing monthly Excel invoice generation the first priority?
2. Is automatic receipt number generation a must-have?
3. Is outstanding fee report a must-have for MVP?
4. Are attendance, teacher, parent portal, and WhatsApp reminder allowed to wait?
5. Is the school willing to test the MVP with real or anonymized student data?
6. Who will approve UAT?
